# CKAI-0005 — Visual Direction V1

## Direction

`PERFECT SURFACE / HOLLOW CORE`: correctness appears as immaculate industrial confidence; understanding is tested by changing conditions and inspecting whether an internal structure remains.

## Material world

- Warm ivory ceramic, smoked optical glass, dark machined metal, restrained champagne gold.
- Photoreal premium industrial/editorial lighting; no dashboard, HUD, network-node or cyberpunk vocabulary.
- No cracks or fracture metaphor. Empty space is revealed through controlled cutaway and withdrawal.

## Motion grammar

1. Pattern assembly: separated physical strips snap into a coherent surface.
2. Context stress: one environmental plate withdraws while external confidence remains.
3. Core reveal: supports retract, then motion stops before the absent center.

The film uses seven short material wipes as visual resets. They are punctuation only and do not substitute for the three semantic hero motions.

## Representation policy

- Pattern: joined strips and weighted layers.
- Context: high-key split plane and changed spatial assumption.
- Core: elongated side cutaway, nested shell, and absent support callback.
- The circular hollow in the original styleframe appears once; later core representations are lateral or oblique.

## Typography contract

- Native Remotion typography; bundled Calibri Bold (`video-factory/animation/src/fonts/calibrib.ttf`) for deterministic Vietnamese glyph coverage.
- Stable content stage: x 64–1004, y 400–1280.
- Uppercase, heavy weight, line-height 1.12, semantic phrase per reveal.
- Champagne gold is reserved for the argumentative pivot, never decorative emphasis.
- Vietnamese glyphs must remain visible at mobile preview scale.
